# Combat feel revision — 29 September 2026

The creator's current Last Stop feedback: sound should feel more arcade-like; some bullets visibly crossed Hype Man but did not hit; dodge moved too fast and its body/shadow relationship did not read as a roll; the HUD should use the Enter the Gungeon screenshot's information hierarchy more closely; and room props should break with different durability. These changes keep Side Quest's approved character and store art, original iconography, cool navy/cyan UI, pink health, and night-out group chat.

## Implemented in the local prototype

- **Sound:** Replaced the eight temporary single-tone cues with short layered arcade sounds. The pistol pops, reload has a two-note store-scanner chirp, dodge scrapes, Hype Man's call rises, and wood/carton and metal shelf impacts have separate hits and breaks. The sounds are generated at startup from original waveforms; no borrowed game audio is used.
- **Projectile readability:** Player and enemy projectiles now test the visible standing/tucked body above each actor's foot origin. Ranged enemies aim at Hype Man's torso rather than feet. The early dodge still ignores bullets; the vulnerable landing remains dangerous. Tests cover a shot through the visible upper body, a genuine miss, and a pistol shot crossing an enemy torso.
- **Dodge:** The first grounded revision reduced roll speed from 270 to 165 native pixels per second. After play, the creator found that it did not travel far enough to escape bullets. The current tuning uses a 225-pixel-per-second base with earlier carry and a softer landing: approximately 79 pixels total over the approved six-pose, 380 ms animation, versus about 55 previously. Invulnerability lasts 250 ms, leaving a vulnerable final 130 ms. The roll shadow follows the lowered body; a separate foot marker that floated under the tumble was removed.
- **HUD:** Health is now six pixel hearts at the upper left, with compact ability readiness beneath. Threat count is at the upper right. The original Pocket Pistol icon, clip count, and cartridge pips stay at the lower right, while reload progress stays over Hype Man's head. The group chat remains a compact branded strip along the bottom. The HUD layout and information priority use the creator's Gungeon screenshot as a reference; icons, colors, wording, and pixel work are original to Side Quest.
- **Breakable cover:** The light carton takes 4 damage, the heavy carton takes 8, and each aisle shelf takes 14. Bullet impacts show damage marks, matching cardboard splinters or metal sparks and sounds. Destroyed cover leaves floor debris and releases its movement/bullet collision. Counter, fridges, fixed perimeter stock, and room boundaries are structural fixtures.
